More than climate change

Air pollution

Exhaust gases and fine particles cause smog and harm our lungs.

Noise

Traffic noise disturbs sleep, health and wildlife.

Land use

Roads and parking lots replace forests and fields, splitting habitats.

Oil and water

Spills and ship waste pollute seas, rivers and coasts.

Cleaner ways to move

Electric vehicles

No exhaust, and even cleaner when charged with green power.

Public transport

One full bus can take dozens of cars off the road.

Walk and cycle

Zero emissions for short trips, and good for your health.

Cleaner fuels

Hydrogen and biofuels for planes, ships and heavy trucks.
